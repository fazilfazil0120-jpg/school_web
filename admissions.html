Step-by-Step Process

How to Apply

Our simple 4-step admission process ensures a smooth experience for parents and students.

1

Enquiry & Registration

Fill out the online enquiry form below or visit our school office. Collect the prospectus and application form.

2

Document Submission

Submit all required documents along with the completed application form and application fee of ₹500.

3

Entrance Assessment

Students appear for a brief entrance assessment (Class VI and above) and an informal interaction with the class teacher.

4

Confirmation & Enrollment

Upon selection, pay the first-term fees to confirm enrollment. Receive your welcome kit and class allocation.

Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply?

  • Child must have completed the age of 5 years by June 1st for Class I admission.
  • For Class VI, student must have passed Class V from a recognized school.
  • For Classes IX and XI, student must have passed the previous class with minimum 50% marks.
  • Students transferring from other boards (CBSE/ICSE) are eligible to apply.
  • Admission is open to students of all castes, creeds, and communities.
  • Siblings of current students get admission priority.
  • Special consideration for students with outstanding sports or cultural achievements.
Required Documents

Documents Needed

  • Birth certificate (original + 2 photocopies)
  • Previous school Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Previous year's Report Card / Mark Sheet
  • Aadhaar Card (child and parents)
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs of student
  • Caste / Community Certificate (if applicable)
  • Proof of residence (any one valid document)
  • Medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor
