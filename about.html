Our Heritage

A Legacy of Excellence

ABC Matriculation Higher Secondary School was founded in 1999 by the visionary educationist, Late Shri. A. Balachandran, with a singular mission — to provide quality education accessible to all. What began as a modest institution with just 150 students and 12 teachers has blossomed into one of the most respected schools in Tamil Nadu.

Today, with over 1,500 students, 100+ faculty members, and state-of-the-art infrastructure spread across a sprawling 5-acre campus, ABC School continues to uphold its founding principles while embracing modern pedagogy and technology.

Our alumni include IIT toppers, IAS officers, entrepreneurs, doctors, and artists — a testament to the well-rounded education we provide. We are affiliated with the Tamil Nadu State Board of Education and recognized by the Government of Tamil Nadu.

Our Journey

School Timeline

A proud chronicle of growth, milestones, and achievements over 25 years.

1999
The Foundation

ABC Matriculation School was established with 150 students and 12 dedicated teachers in a modest two-story building. The dream of quality education for all was born.

2003
Higher Secondary Addition

Classes 11 and 12 (Higher Secondary) were introduced with Science and Commerce streams. First batch of 11th grade graduated with 98% pass rate.

2007
New Campus & Infrastructure

Moved to the current 5-acre campus. New buildings, computer lab, science labs, and a library wing were inaugurated. Student strength crossed 500.

2012
Digital Learning Initiative

Launched smart classrooms with interactive whiteboards and digital content. Became one of the first schools in the district to adopt e-learning.

2016
Sports Complex & Auditorium

State-of-the-art sports complex and 1,000-seat auditorium were inaugurated. School began participating in national-level sports competitions.

2020
Digital Resilience – COVID Era

Successfully transitioned to hybrid learning during the pandemic. Launched ABC School's online learning portal ensuring uninterrupted education.

2024
25th Anniversary – Silver Jubilee

Celebrated our 25th anniversary with a grand gathering of alumni, parents, and dignitaries. Launched the ABC School Foundation for merit scholarships.

2026
Today & Beyond

1,500+ students, 100+ faculty, world-class infrastructure, and a proud legacy of excellence. The journey continues — stronger than ever.
