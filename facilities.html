Infrastructure

Everything Students Need

Our 5-acre campus is equipped with modern facilities that create the ideal learning environment.

💻
Technology

Smart Classrooms

All 50+ classrooms are equipped with state-of-the-art interactive smart boards, high-definition projectors, digital learning platforms, and individual student response systems. Every classroom is Wi-Fi enabled with fiber-optic internet connectivity. Our digital curriculum is integrated with the TN State Board syllabus, making lessons more engaging and effective.

50+ Smart Classrooms Interactive Whiteboards HD Projectors Wi-Fi Connected Digital Curriculum AC Classrooms
🖥️
Digital Learning

Computer Laboratory

Two fully equipped computer labs with 80 high-performance workstations each. Students learn programming, web development, graphic design, and digital literacy. Labs are equipped with the latest software including Python IDEs, Microsoft Office Suite, Adobe Creative Suite, and educational platforms. Both labs are air-conditioned and available for extended hours.

160 Workstations Python & Java High-Speed Internet Air Conditioned 3D Printing Robotics Kit
🔬
Science & Research

Science Laboratories

Three separate, fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Each lab has modern instruments, digital equipment, and safety features conforming to national standards. Students conduct hands-on experiments aligned with the TN Board curriculum. The Biology lab includes advanced microscopes, preserved specimens, and plant growth chambers.

Physics Lab Chemistry Lab Biology Lab Modern Instruments Safety Equipment Digital Microscopes
📚
Knowledge Hub

Central Library

Our spacious, well-lit central library houses over 20,000 books spanning all subjects, fiction, non-fiction, reference books, encyclopedias, and magazines. The library is digitally catalogued for easy access and includes e-book subscriptions, audio-visual resources, and quiet reading rooms. Students can borrow up to 5 books simultaneously.

20,000+ Books E-Book Access Digital Catalogue Reading Rooms Magazines & Journals Research Corner
⚽
Sports & Health

Sports Complex & Playground

A large multi-purpose sports complex and outdoor playground spanning 1.5 acres. Facilities include a cricket ground, football field, basketball courts, volleyball courts, badminton courts, and a 400-meter running track. An indoor sports hall accommodates table tennis, chess, and carrom. Students are guided by certified coaches.

Cricket Ground Football Field Basketball Court 400m Track Indoor Sports Hall Certified Coaches
🎭
Arts & Culture

Auditorium

A magnificent 1,200-seat air-conditioned auditorium that serves as the venue for annual days, cultural programs, guest lectures, and inter-school competitions. Equipped with a professional sound system, LED stage lighting, a large LED display screen, backstage rooms, and modern acoustics. It serves as the cultural heart of our school.

1,200 Seats Air Conditioned Pro Sound System LED Stage Lights LED Display Backstage Rooms
🚌
Transport

Transportation

A safe and reliable fleet of 12 GPS-tracked school buses covers 35+ routes across major areas of Chennai. All buses are maintained to highest safety standards with trained drivers and attendants. Each bus is equipped with CCTV cameras. Real-time bus tracking is available via the ABC School parent app.

12 School Buses 35+ Routes GPS Tracked CCTV in Buses Trained Drivers Parent App Tracking
🍽️
Nutrition

School Canteen

A hygienic, spacious canteen serving nutritious, freshly prepared meals and snacks. Our menu is designed by a certified nutritionist to support growing children's health. We serve a variety of South Indian, North Indian, and healthy international options. The canteen is FSSAI certified and follows strict hygiene protocols.

FSSAI Certified Nutritionist Menu Fresh Daily Meals Hygienic Kitchen Spacious Seating Affordable Pricing
📹
Safety & Security

CCTV & Security

Comprehensive 24/7 security infrastructure with 150+ HD CCTV cameras covering every corner of the campus — classrooms, corridors, entry/exit points, playgrounds, and buses. A dedicated security control room monitors feeds in real-time. Biometric entry for staff, photo ID cards for students, and visitor management system ensure maximum safety.

150+ CCTV Cameras 24/7 Monitoring Biometric Entry Security Guards Visitor Management Emergency Alerts
